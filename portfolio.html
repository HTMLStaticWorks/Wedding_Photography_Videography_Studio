Our Approach

Understanding our shooting style and editorial capabilities.

Editorial Portraits

We blend fine-art fashion aesthetics with genuine emotion. We gently direct you to find the most flattering light and angles, ensuring a comfortable experience while producing magazine-worthy imagery.

Candid Photojournalism

For the majority of your day, we step back and document the beautiful, unscripted moments as they unfold naturally. We capture the laughter, the tears, and the fleeting connections that make your story unique.

Our Process

Wedding Day Protocol

A structured approach to ensure perfect documentation. From preparation to the final send-off, we meticulously plan our coverage.

01

Pre-Wedding Consultation

Always verify the final timeline and shot list with our team before the big day to ensure a smooth, stress-free experience.

02

Details & Getting Ready

We start the day capturing the intricate details of your attire, accessories, and the anticipation of getting ready with your loved ones.

03

The First Look & Portraits

An intimate moment for just the two of you, followed by relaxed editorial portraits that capture your authentic connection.

04

Ceremony & Reception

Discreet, photojournalistic coverage of your vows, first dances, speeches, and the grand celebration with your guests.
